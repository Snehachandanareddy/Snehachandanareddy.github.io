01 / About

My Story

From systems administration to automation, working my way into DevOps from Brisbane.

My path into tech started with a Bachelor's in Computer Science at B.V. Raju Institute of Technology in India. After graduating I wanted to go deeper, so in 2021 I moved to Brisbane to pursue a Master of Information Technology at Griffith University. Australia has been home ever since.

Since finishing my Master's I've been on the operational side of technology, currently supporting frontline officers across the Queensland Police Service and Queensland Ambulance Service. Three years close to live production systems has shaped how I think about software. I've watched bugs surface at the worst possible moments and learned how much a clear error message or a sensible default is worth.

Now I'm moving into DevOps. At work I've worked on a PowerShell tool that automates user account creation and built Power Automate flows that remove daily manual tasks. Outside work I build CI/CD pipelines in Jenkins with Maven, GitHub and Tomcat, and I'm sharpening my fundamentals so I can move into a DevOps role full-time.

Outside of work I enjoy long walks around Brisbane, cooking the food I grew up on, and the occasional weekend road trip up the coast.
